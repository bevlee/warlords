# Warlords generated icon prompt set

## Shared art direction

All icons were generated as square fantasy strategy-game pixel art, matched to
the existing Warlords creature sprite sheets. Each uses a centered silhouette,
crisp stepped edges, a dark navy-black outline, restrained highlights, no text,
no frame, and a flat chroma-key background removed after generation. Attribute
icons are intentionally the simplest family; statuses use medium detail; items
have the richest material detail. Final assets are transparent 128×128 PNGs.

- `mana`: one faceted blue arcane crystal drop.
- `xp`: a compact violet-white rising sparkle.
- `count`: two simple overlapping steel-blue helmets.
- `hp`: one bold red heart with a small warm highlight.
- `attack`: two crossed steel swords with brown grips.
- `defense`: one compact blue-steel heater shield.
- `damage`: one flat golden-yellow irregular impact burst, based on the supplied
  yellow-burst reference and translated into the game's outlined pixel style.
- `speed`: one tan winged boot angled forward.
- `initiative`: one sharp cyan lightning bolt.
- `range`: one red-and-cream archery target with a centered arrow.
- `shots`: one small quiver holding three gold-tipped arrows.
- `morale`: one upright brass war horn with a red binding.
- `luck`: one clean emerald four-leaf clover.
- `cost`: a short stack of bright gold coins.

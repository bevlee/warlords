Necromancer Artifacts

20 artifacts across starting, common, rare, and epic tiers.

ATB means progress toward a unit’s next turn.

Starting artifact 1

Gravewright's Grimoire

When an enemy stack perishes, it raises temporary Skeletons from 10% of its starting total HP.

Common 8

Marrow Crown

Gravewrights Grimoire Skeletons arrive halfway to their first turn instead of waiting the full cycle.

Plague Bell

Gravewright’s Grimoire raises twice as many creatures from enemies afflicted by Infecting Strike.

Wailing Lantern

Drain Morale removes 2 Morale instead of 1.

Crimson Needle

Blood Frenzy grants +4 damage per wound instead of +2.

Chalice of Night

Vampire Life Drain heals 150% of damage dealt instead of 100% when attacking a target with an Affliction.

Withered Quiver

Curse Shot also applies −5 Defense.

Reaper's Tack

Soul Reaper claims 2 additional creatures.

Vertebral Key

After Bone Dragon uses Absorb Skeleton, it returns at 50% ATB.

Rare 7

Book of Grudges

Every stack you own deals 15% more damage per Affliction on the target.

Blighted Soil

When an enemy stack carrying Infecting Strike perishes, it spreads Infecting Strike to adjacent enemies.

Blood Tithe

Every 6 HP of Life Drain healing beyond full raises a Skeleton.

Funeral Drum

For every 5 friendly Skeletons lost, every living non-Skeleton Necromancer stack advances 10% ATB. Leftovers carry over.

Empty Throne

Enemies at −3 Morale cannot retaliate and take 50% more damage.

Knight's Reliquary

Soul Reaper kills rise as Skeletons.

Shroud of Preservation

When a friendly Skeleton stack perishes, half the creatures it had immediately before the killing blow, rounded down, join your largest surviving Skeleton stack.

Epic 4

Dragon Ossuary

Gravewrights Grimoire raises Bone Dragons instead of Skeletons.

Crown of Ruin

Infecting Strike, Curse and Morale Drain apply twice.

Red Moon Covenant

Blood Frenzy’s per-wound damage bonus and Life Drain healing are doubled.

The Black Procession

Every friendly Skeleton permanently gains Infecting Strike and Drain Morale.
